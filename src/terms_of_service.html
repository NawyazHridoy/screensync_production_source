1. Purpose & Digital Wellness Scope

ScreenSync provides self-regulation, screen-time awareness, short-form video scroll tracking, focus timers, sound safety monitoring, and cross-device usage synchronization. ScreenSync is a personal productivity tool and is not a medical device.
